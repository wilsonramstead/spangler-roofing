Why Late Fall and Winter Is the Best Time to Replace Your Roof in Tampa Bay

November 10, 2026 · By Spangler Roofing LLC · Serving Florida since 1989

Most people replace their roof when they have to: right after a leak, right after a storm, or right after a letter from the insurance company. But if you know your roof is getting near the end, there's a best time of year to do it here in Tampa Bay, and it's right now.

1. It's the dry season

From roughly November through spring, the afternoon thunderstorms that hit almost daily all summer mostly go away. That matters more than people think. A reroof means tearing off the old roof down to the decking, and every day that decking is open is a day you want clear skies. Dry weather means fewer rain delays, less waiting, and a smoother job from tear-off to the last shingle.

2. Hurricane season is over

Atlantic hurricane season officially ends November 30 and doesn't start again until June 1. Replacing your roof in the off-season means the job gets done without a storm in the forecast, and you go into next season with a brand-new roof instead of crossing your fingers on an old one.

3. Scheduling is easier

After a storm, every roofer in Florida is booked solid and homeowners wait weeks. In spring, a lot of people who put it off all winter suddenly call at once. Late fall and winter is the quieter stretch in between. You have a better shot at the date you want, and more time to talk through your options without feeling rushed.

4. It's easier on your house and your crew

Cooler temperatures make for safer, steadier work on the roof. And if you're switching materials, say from shingles to metal or tile, the extra planning time lets you make that decision carefully instead of under pressure.

5. It can help with your insurance

A new roof built to current Florida code can make your home easier to insure, and an updated wind mitigation report afterward may lower the windstorm part of your premium. Getting the roof done before your next renewal means you can send your insurer the paperwork when it counts. We covered this in more detail in our post on roof age and insurance in Florida.

Signs your roof might be due

  • The roof is around 15 to 20 years old (shingles) or the tile underlayment has never been replaced
  • Shingles are curling, cracking or missing, or you're finding granules in the gutters
  • You've had more than one leak, or stains on the ceiling keep coming back
  • The roof made it through a storm, but you're not sure it'll make it through another one
  • Your insurance company is asking about the roof's age or condition

What to expect

It starts with a free inspection and estimate. If a replacement makes sense, we pull the permit, schedule the job, and handle everything from tear-off to final cleanup, including a magnetic nail sweep of your yard and driveway. Most homes are done in just a few days once work starts.

If your roof has been on your mind, this is the window to take care of it. Call us and we'll come take a look. If it still has life left, we'll tell you that too.

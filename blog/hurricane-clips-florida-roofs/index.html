Hurricane Clips: The Small Metal Connector That Can Save Your Home During a Storm

July 28, 2026 · By Spangler Roofing LLC · Serving Florida since 1989

When most homeowners think about protecting their home from hurricanes, they focus on impact windows, shutters, or a new roof. While those are all important, one of the most critical components of your home's structural defense is something most people never see: hurricane clips.

What are hurricane clips?

Hurricane clips are heavy-duty metal connectors that secure your roof structure directly to your home's walls. Their primary purpose is to help keep your roof attached to your house during high winds.

Without these connectors, hurricane-force winds can create tremendous uplift pressure, potentially lifting the roof off the home. Hurricane clips significantly increase the home's ability to withstand these forces.

Why are hurricane clips so important?

Florida experiences some of the strongest wind events in the country. During a hurricane, winds don't just push against your home — they also pull upward on your roof. A properly installed roof with hurricane clips provides several important benefits:

  • Increased protection against roof uplift
  • Better structural integrity during severe storms
  • Reduced risk of catastrophic roof failure
  • Improved safety for your family
  • Potential discounts on homeowners insurance through wind mitigation inspections

Older homes may not have them

Many homes built before modern building codes were adopted either have no hurricane clips or have outdated connectors that don't meet today's standards. If your home was built decades ago, it's worth having a professional inspection to determine whether hurricane clips are present — and whether upgrades are possible during a reroofing project.

Hurricane clips during roof replacement

One of the best opportunities to inspect or upgrade hurricane clips is during a roof replacement. Once the existing roofing materials are removed, we can access the roof-to-wall connection and determine if additional reinforcement is needed. Installing or upgrading hurricane clips during a reroof can provide long-term protection and may help your home better withstand future storms.

Insurance benefits

Many Florida homeowners qualify for insurance savings through a Wind Mitigation Inspection. One of the major items inspectors evaluate is the roof-to-wall connection. Homes with properly installed hurricane clips or straps often receive credits that can help lower annual insurance premiums.

Protect your biggest investment

Hurricane clips may be hidden behind the walls and roof decking, but they play one of the biggest roles in protecting your property during severe storms. We've been helping Florida homeowners protect their homes since 1989 — if you're planning a roof replacement or want to know whether your home has adequate hurricane protection, we're here to help.
